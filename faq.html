FAQ

The full list

We already use Asana. Why isn’t it working?

Most companies on Asana have the tool, not the system. There are projects but no structure: ownership is unclear, escalations are unmanaged and leadership can’t see anything without asking. We set Asana up around your decision flows and accountability, so it becomes a workspace your team uses instead of one they work around.

How long does setup take for an e-commerce brand?

A complete, custom Asana setup usually takes about 30 days from audit to launch. That covers mapping your current workflows, building portfolios and automated rules, and live training so the whole team can run it on their own.

Why not just hire a fractional COO?

A fractional COO typically costs $8,000–$15,000 a month. They give good strategic advice but rarely build your workspace, document your processes or train your team. We build the permanent infrastructure that makes a fractional COO unnecessary at this stage, for a fraction of the annual cost.

What if my team resists the new system?

Adoption is built in from day one. We design workflows around how your team already communicates, roll out in phases people can absorb, and train everyone to the level their role needs. Before we hand over, we agree who inside your team owns adoption, so the system keeps running after we step back.

Do you work with tools other than Asana?

Yes. Asana is where we go deepest, and we also build complete systems in ClickUp: workload views, automated pipelines and reporting. Around either one we integrate Slack, Google Workspace, Notion and reporting tools, and we’ve moved teams off ClickUp, Trello and Airtable into one Asana workspace when that was the better fit. The Ops X-Ray maps the right path for your stack.

What happens on the free 20-minute call?

You tell us what’s breaking and we ask how work moves today. By the end we’ll point out where the bottleneck is and who should own it. If there’s a fit, we’ll usually suggest the 5-day Ops X-Ray, whose fee is credited to the build. If there isn’t, we’ll say so.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything is built inside your own Asana or ClickUp workspace, and every build is handed over with a written SOP and a video guide. There’s no lock-in.

What exactly do you guarantee?

Three things on the 30-Day Brand OS. If you don’t like the plan we show you on day 7, you get a full refund and keep the plan. If the system isn’t live by day 30, we keep building at no cost. If fewer than 80% of your team are using it weekly by day 60, we keep working free for up to 30 more days, as long as you’ve named an internal owner and the team attended two trainings.
