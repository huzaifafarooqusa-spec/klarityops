01

Brand idea

Founders don’t hire us for Asana. They hire us to get the business out of their head and into a system their team can run. Everything in the brand should make things feel clearer, calmer and more in control.

Clarity you can run a company on.Our promise, in one line. Use it as the north star, not necessarily as copy.

Clear

Say it plainly. One idea per sentence, one message per section. If a founder can’t scan it in five seconds, rewrite it.

Calm

We’re the steady operator in a chaotic business. No hype, no fake urgency, no exclamation marks.

Proven

Show the real thing: real Asana builds, real client numbers, real people. Never mockups pretending to be work.

Human

Founder-to-founder. We talk like the people who’ve sat in the chaos, because we have.

02

Logo

The mark is a K built from two parts: a solid bar (the structure) and a checkmark (work getting done). The checkmark is always highlighter yellow.

Primary. On white and light backgrounds.

Reversed. On navy, photos and dark UI.

Mark only. Avatars, favicons, stickers, tight spaces.

Name

Always KlarityOps: one word, capital K and O. Not “Klarity Ops”, “klarityops” or “Klarity-Ops” in copy.

Space and size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the “K” bar on every side. Minimum size: 24px mark, 110px full logo width on screen.

Don’t

✕

Rotate or tilt it

✕

Recolour the mark

✕

Stretch or squash

✕

Place on yellow or busy images

04

Typography

Two families, both free for commercial use from Fontshare. Headlines have character; body text stays out of the way.

Aa

Cabinet Grotesk

Headlines, numbers, the wordmark. ExtraBold (800) for headings, Bold (700) for card titles.

Aa

Satoshi

Body copy, buttons, labels, forms. Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700).

HeroCabinet 800 · 56–72px · 1.03
Stop running your brand from your head.
Section headingCabinet 800 · 36–50px · 1.08
Does your week look like this?
Card titleCabinet 700 · 20–24px
Deadlines keep slipping
BodySatoshi 400 · 17–18px · 1.6
We build Asana operating systems for D2C brands doing $5M–$100M. Every workstream gets an owner.
LabelSatoshi 700 · 15px · Blue
How it works
  • Sentence case everywhere. No ALL CAPS labels, no letter-spaced micro text.
  • Keep lines to about 65 characters for body copy.
  • Numbers are the heroes of proof: set them big in Cabinet, with a short plain label underneath.

06

Voice

Write like a senior operator talking to a founder over coffee: direct, specific and a little warm. Our best lines come from what clients actually say.

Not like this
Like this
Unlock seamless operational excellence with AI-powered synergy.
Every task gets an owner, and you stop chasing updates.
We leverage cutting-edge solutions to empower your team.
We build it inside your Asana. Your team runs it.
Revolutionize your workflow today!!!
Book a free 20-minute call.
  • Use real numbers only (66% less blocker time, 50+ brands, 30 days). Never round up or invent.
  • Words to avoid: seamless, unlock, leverage, empower, synergy, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionize.
  • Keep taglines short and spoken: “Stop running your brand from your head.” “Get the business out of your head.”
07

Imagery

Real beats pretty. Our visuals prove the work is real.

Real Asana builds

Screenshots and screen recordings from actual workspaces, shown in a simple browser frame. Blur client names and sensitive data.

Real people

Team portraits and talking-head videos from the team. Warm light, dark clothing, simple backgrounds.

Never

No stock photos, no AI-generated people or offices, no fake dashboards, no glowing robot brains.
