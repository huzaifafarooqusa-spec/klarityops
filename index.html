FAQ

You have questions. We have answers.

Is this for my stage of business?

If you are a D2C or E-commerce founder doing $1M–$5M with 8-20 people, and you are currently the bottleneck for every decision, yes. If you are under $500k, you likely need a virtual assistant, not a systemic overhaul.

Take your life back. Book your demo today.

What if my team refuses to use the new system?

Tool adoption fails because the system is confusing or feels like extra work. We design the system *around* how your team already communicates, minimizing friction. We also lead the training to ensure everyone knows exactly what's expected of them.

Stop fighting your tools. Let us build one they actually use.

Why shouldn't I just hire a Fractional COO?

A Fractional COO costs $5,000–$10,000 a month. They give strategic advice, but they rarely get their hands dirty building your Asana workspace. We build the infrastructure that makes a COO unnecessary at this stage, for a fraction of the cost.

Get the system, save the retainer. Book a call.

How long does this take to see results?

The entire sprint is 30 days. You will get extreme clarity in Week 1 from the Ops Diagnostic. By Week 4, your team is fully operating inside the new Asana environment, and you can step back immediately.

Your freedom is 30 days away. Start the sprint.

What is the catch?

The catch is that you actually have to let go. We will build a flawless system, but if you continue to answer 9pm WhatsApp messages instead of redirecting them to Asana, the chaos will return. We build the house; you have to lock the front door.
