Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Fractional BizOps Consultant?
A Fractional Business Operations (BizOps) Consultant acts as an embedded operations leader on a part-time or project basis. Unlike traditional consultants who only offer advice, we physically build the infrastructure, configure the tools (like Asana), and train your team, providing executive-level operational scaling at a fraction of the cost of a full-time COO.
We already use Asana. Why isn't it working?
Most companies using Asana have the tool, not the system. They have projects, but no architecture. Tasks exist but ownership is ambiguous, escalations are unmanaged, and leadership has no visibility. We don't set up Asana — we engineer it around your org's specific decision flows and accountability structure. The result is a workspace your team actually uses, not one they work around.
How long does it take to set up Asana for an e-commerce brand?
A complete, custom Asana architecture typically takes 30 days from audit to deployment. This includes mapping your current workflows, engineering the portfolios and automated rules, and running live adoption training for your entire team so they can execute independently.
Why shouldn't I just hire a Fractional COO?
A Fractional COO typically costs $8,000–$15,000 a month. They give excellent strategic advice, but they rarely get into the weeds to build your Asana workspace, document your processes, or train your team on execution systems. We build the permanent infrastructure that makes a fractional COO unnecessary at this stage — for a one-time fraction of the annual cost.
