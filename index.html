The problem

Three problems every $20M–$100M brand hits.

Different symptoms. Same cause: nobody can see the whole launch until it slips.

  1. 01

    Leadership finds out in Monday’s meeting.

    Status lives in heads, threads and spreadsheets. By the time a slip reaches the leadership meeting, the fix costs a week.

    What you hear
    • “Quick one, which version did we approve?”
    • “Can you confirm the ship date?”
    • Weekly status sync · 8 guests · 60 min
  2. 02

    Launches slip between teams.

    Every department owns its piece, nobody owns the handoff. One missed launch costs six figures in lost sales, stock and ad spend.

    What you hear
    • “Can you approve this PO? Need it today.”
    • Low stock: your best seller
    • “The inserts are wrong again.”
  3. 03

    More people and tools haven’t made it easier.

    Each hire and each new tool adds questions, not answers. Department heads build their own systems, and nobody sees across them.

    What you hear
    • Overdue tasks nobody opened
    • “My last day is the 30th.”
    • “Away until Monday. Reachable on WhatsApp.”

FAQ

Questions we hear on every call

Is this for my brand?

It fits best if you run a DTC brand doing $20M to $100M a year, with 40 to 200 people and department heads, and leadership wants every launch visible without another status meeting. Under $20M, start with our free kits. Not a fit if you want hourly help on one task.

We already use Asana or ClickUp. Why isn’t it working?

Most brands have the tool, not the system. There are projects but no owners, no dates and no weekly scorecard, so each team goes back to its own tracker and leadership finds out late. We set the tool up around who decides what, so it answers the questions you answer today.

What exactly do you guarantee?

Three things on the 30-Day Launch OS, for Asana and ClickUp builds. If you don’t like the plan we show you on day 7, you get a full refund and keep the plan. If the system isn’t live by day 30, we keep building at no cost. If fewer than 80% of your team are using it weekly by day 60, we keep working free for up to 30 more days, as long as you’ve named an internal owner and the team attended two trainings.

What happens on the free 20-minute call?

You tell us where launches slip, and we ask how work moves between teams today. By the end we’ll point out the biggest leaks and the first three things to fix. If there’s a fit, we’ll usually suggest the 5-day Ops X-Ray, whose fee is credited to the build. If there isn’t, we’ll say so.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything is built inside your own Asana or ClickUp workspace, and every build is handed over with a written SOP and a video guide. There’s no lock-in.
