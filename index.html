FAQ

Questions we hear on every call

We already use Asana. Why isn’t it working?

Most companies on Asana have the tool, not the system. There are projects but no structure: ownership is unclear, escalations are unmanaged and leadership can’t see anything without asking. We set Asana up around your decision flows and accountability, so it becomes a workspace your team uses instead of one they work around.

How long does setup take for an e-commerce brand?

A complete, custom Asana setup usually takes about 30 days from audit to launch. That covers mapping your current workflows, building portfolios and automated rules, and live training so the whole team can run it on their own.

What happens on the free 20-minute call?

You tell us what’s breaking and we ask how work moves today. By the end we’ll point out where the bottleneck is and who should own it. If there’s a fit, we’ll recommend a starting point, usually the Systems Audit, whose fee is credited to your first build. If there isn’t, we’ll say so.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything is built inside your own Asana or ClickUp workspace, and every build is handed over with a written SOP and a video guide. There’s no lock-in: keep the retainer only while it’s useful.
