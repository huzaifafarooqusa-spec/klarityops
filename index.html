FAQ

Questions we hear on every call

Is this for my brand?

It fits best if you run a DTC brand doing roughly $5M to $25M a year, with a team of about 8 to 50 people, and you’re still the person most questions and approvals go to. It’s not a fit if you’re a solo founder, or if you want hourly help on one task. We’ll say so on the call.

We already use Asana or ClickUp. Why isn’t it working?

Most brands have the tool, not the system. There are projects but no owners, no dates and no weekly scorecard, so the team goes back to DMs and every question comes back to you. We set the tool up around who decides what, so it answers the questions you answer today.

What exactly do you guarantee?

Three things on the 30-Day Brand OS. If you don’t like the plan we show you on day 7, you get a full refund and keep the plan. If the system isn’t live by day 30, we keep building at no cost. If fewer than 80% of your team are using it weekly by day 60, we keep working free for up to 30 more days, as long as you’ve named an internal owner and the team attended two trainings.

What happens on the free 20-minute call?

You tell us what still lands on you, and we ask how work moves today. By the end we’ll point out where the business runs through you and the first three things to fix. If there’s a fit, we’ll usually suggest the 5-day Ops X-Ray, whose fee is credited to the build. If there isn’t, we’ll say so.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything is built inside your own Asana or ClickUp workspace, and every build is handed over with a written SOP and a video guide. There’s no lock-in.
