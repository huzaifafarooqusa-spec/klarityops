The Problem

Your business outgrew the way it was run.

This is not a people problem. It is a missing system. The symptoms below are what a missing operating layer looks like once revenue scales past it.

Software Sprawl & Broken Handoffs

The team is subscribed to Slack, Notion, Asana, and ClickUp, but execution is fragmented. Workflows are isolated, handoffs are dropped, and no single person owns the pipeline end-to-end. This is a classic e-commerce operational bottleneck.

The Executive Blind Spot

You're managing by Slack DM and guessing on capacity. Leadership cannot see accurate execution status without chasing down updates or scheduling a 45-minute sync. Proactive management is replaced by constant reactive firefighting.

The $10M Glass Ceiling

What got you here is breaking your fulfillment times now. Your processes worked perfectly at $1M/year. At $10M/year, the informal handoffs and tribal knowledge systems fracture under load, presenting major D2C scaling challenges.

The Ultimate Bottleneck

Founder-Centric Friction

Every critical decision, quality assurance check, and strategic launch routes through the founder by default. The business is structurally dependent on its heaviest bottleneck, and growth stalls exactly where the founder's calendar ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Fractional BizOps Consultant?
A Fractional Business Operations (BizOps) Consultant acts as an embedded operations leader on a part-time or project basis. Unlike traditional consultants who only offer advice, we physically build the infrastructure, configure the tools (like Asana), and train your team, providing executive-level operational scaling at a fraction of the cost of a full-time COO.
We already use Asana. Why isn't it working?
Most companies using Asana have the tool, not the system. They have projects, but no architecture. Tasks exist but ownership is ambiguous, escalations are unmanaged, and leadership has no visibility. We don't set up Asana. We engineer it around your org's specific decision flows and accountability structure. The result is a workspace your team actually uses, not one they work around.
How long does it take to set up Asana for an e-commerce brand?
A complete, custom Asana architecture typically takes 30 days from audit to deployment. This includes mapping your current workflows, engineering the portfolios and automated rules, and running live adoption training for your entire team so they can execute independently.
Why shouldn't I just hire a Fractional COO?
A Fractional COO typically costs $8,000–$15,000 a month. They give excellent strategic advice, but they rarely get into the weeds to build your Asana workspace, document your processes, or train your team on execution systems. We build the permanent infrastructure that makes a fractional COO unnecessary at this stage, at a fraction of the annual cost.
What if my team resists the new system?
Adoption isn't an afterthought here. It's engineered in from day one. We build workflows around how your team already communicates, ship the system in phased rollouts your people can absorb, and train every person to the fluency level their seat requires. That's why our case studies show 100% team adoption and zero reversion to Slack-only execution after 90 days.
Do you work with tools other than Asana?
Asana is the backbone of every operating system we build. It's where we're certified and where we go deepest. But your stack rarely stops there. We routinely integrate Slack, Google Workspace, Notion, and reporting layers, and we've migrated teams off ClickUp, Trello, and Airtable into consolidated Asana workspaces. If you're on another platform today, the Systems Audit is the place to map the path over.
