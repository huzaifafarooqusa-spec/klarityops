Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this for my stage of business?
If you are a scaling e-com brand doing $1M–$5M with 8-20 people, and you are the bottleneck, yes. If you are under $500k in revenue, you likely need a virtual assistant to take on basic tasks, not a systemic overhaul yet.
What if my team refuses to use it?
Tool adoption fails when systems are confusing or add friction. We design the architecture around how your team already communicates (e.g., deep Slack integrations) so updating tasks feels natural. We also lead the live training to ensure zero confusion on day one.
Why shouldn't I just hire a Fractional COO?
A Fractional COO typically costs $5,000–$10,000 a month. They give excellent strategic advice, but they rarely get into the weeds to build your Asana workspace. We build the permanent infrastructure that makes a COO unnecessary at this stage, for a one-time fraction of the cost.
