ClickUp questions

Before you book

Our ClickUp is a mess. Do we start over?

Usually not. We keep what works, merge duplicate statuses and lists, and archive the rest. Your team keeps working while we rebuild around owners and dates.

Should we be on Asana instead?

If your team already lives in ClickUp, we build there. A tool switch costs months of adoption. We only suggest moving if something you need truly isn’t possible in ClickUp.

Do we need a paid ClickUp plan?

Custom fields and automations have limits on the free plan. We tell you on the call what your build needs and whether an upgrade is worth it.
