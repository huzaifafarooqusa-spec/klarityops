Before you book

We already use Asana. Why isn’t it working?

Most brands have Asana, not a system in Asana. Projects exist, but there are no owners, no dates and no weekly scorecard, so the team goes back to Slack. We build around who decides what, so Asana answers the questions that come to you today.

Do we need a higher Asana plan?

Not always. We build on the plan you have and tell you on the call if something you need sits on a higher tier, and whether it’s worth paying for.

Can you move us from Sheets, Notion, Monday or ClickUp?

Yes. Migration of active work is part of the 30-Day Brand OS. We move what’s live, archive what isn’t, and run both side by side for a short overlap so nothing drops.
